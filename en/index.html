02 — Brand Essence

Every taste is different.
Our care is always the same.

Beauty has its own gyeol — its own grain. At THE SONGYEOL, we believe that caring for hands and feet deserves the same thoughtful welcome a boutique hotel prepares for one guest.

Before we begin, we ask about your day, your taste, the care you want and the time you have. Our specialists confirm the details with you and choose the right treatment and approach together.

Today's colour and length, the posture that felt comfortable, the small feedback you shared — all of it carries into your next visit. A brand where the conversation simply continues. That is THE SONGYEOL.

03 — Signature Care System

One visit.
Hands and feet, together.

For people with full schedules who still want well-kept hands and feet, we design single, sequential or simultaneous care around you and the service.

  1. 01

    60–90 seconds

    Care Check

    Before your treatment, a 60–90 second check covers your purpose, available time, design taste and any care you'd like to add. It is a conversation to choose the right care — not a medical diagnosis.

  2. 02

    Nail + Foot Care

    NAIL & FOOT RELAX

    While your manicure is done, a foot-care device eases tired feet. Waiting time becomes rest time.

  3. 03

    Duo Care

    NAIL & PEDI DUO

    A nail specialist and a pedicure specialist care for you together, overlapping only where it keeps you comfortable and the result precise.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    Record & Return

    We record today's colour, length, comfort and feedback so your next visit picks up where this one left off.

* NAIL & FOOT RELAX and NAIL & PEDI DUO will be introduced step by step after in-store validation. Availability is confirmed with your specialist during consultation.
