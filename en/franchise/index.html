Operating System

Six systems
that run every store.

01

Brand Standard

Space & visual guide

Colour ratio (oat white 55 · orange 20 · stainless 15 · charcoal 10), arch motif, lighting and wordmark rules.

02

Hospitality SOP

Five moments

Prepare · Welcome · Attend · Finish · Remember. A standard for every touchpoint.

03

Care Check

60–90 seconds

A short pre-treatment check that reduces decision pressure and time uncertainty.

04

Care Record

Five fields

Service code · look & product code · length & shape · keep/change feedback · one thing to check next time.

05

Academy

Skills & service training

Nail and pedicure technique taught together with hospitality standards, with regular quality checks.

06

AI Care Concierge

In planning

Care-check summaries, suggestion support and record keeping. Not a diagnostic tool — people decide.
